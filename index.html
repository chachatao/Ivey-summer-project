Scotiabank Interest Rate-Related Products Map

A structured overview of Scotiabank Canada product families where interest rates affect customer value, borrowing cost, competitive comparison, and potential market sentiment.

Scope Logic

Interest rate-related products are broader than mortgages. They include products where customers earn interest, pay interest, compare rates with competitors, or react to rate changes in the broader market.

Earn Interest Savings accounts, registered cash, investment cash, and business interest-bearing accounts.
Lock In Return GICs, cashable GICs, redeemable GICs, and market-linked GICs.
Pay Interest Mortgages, loans, auto financing, lines of credit, and credit cards.
Track Benchmarks Prime rate, reference rates, Bank of Canada rate, and US Federal Funds target rate.

1. Mortgages

Variable Rate Mortgage

Directly linked to prime-rate movement.

  • Strong fit for tracking customer reaction to rate cuts or hikes.
  • Useful for sentiment around affordability, payment uncertainty, and renewal decisions.
  • Clear connection to Scotiabank prime rate and competitor variable-rate offers.

Fixed Rate Mortgage

Driven by posted rates, bond yields, and rate expectations.

  • Important for fixed vs variable customer decision-making.
  • Common topic in renewal, switching, and home affordability discussions.
  • Easy to compare against other major Canadian banks.

Short-Term Fixed Mortgage

Useful when customers expect future rate changes.

  • Relevant to discussions about waiting for lower rates.
  • More niche than standard fixed and variable mortgages.
  • Can be included later as a filter or sub-segment.

Scotia Total Equity Plan (STEP)

Home equity borrowing structure.

  • Relevant to rate pressure and home-equity borrowing.
  • Public discussion may be less direct than core mortgage products.
  • Better as a later expansion topic.

Special Mortgage Program

Offer-specific and context-dependent.

  • Potentially useful when promotions or targeted programs are active.
  • May not have enough consistent public discussion for the first dashboard version.
  • Best treated as an event or campaign monitor.

2. GICs

Non-Redeemable GIC

Classic fixed-rate, term-based savings product.

  • Strongest fit for rate competitiveness analysis.
  • Customers commonly compare term, posted rate, and competitor offers.
  • Clear sentiment topics: attractive rate, locked-in term, renewal, opportunity cost.

Cashable or Redeemable GIC

Trade-off between flexibility and return.

  • Useful for analyzing flexibility vs higher-rate trade-offs.
  • Good contrast with non-redeemable GICs.
  • Relevant when customers are uncertain about future liquidity needs.

Market Linked GIC

Principal protection plus market-linked return potential.

  • Interesting for sentiment around complexity, upside, and guarantees.
  • Less directly comparable than standard GIC rates.
  • Better suited for a later, more investment-focused dashboard view.

Special GIC Offers

Promotional rate campaigns.

  • Useful for monitoring short-term spikes in attention.
  • Can be highly relevant during campaign periods.
  • Better treated as an event layer rather than a stable core product.

Guaranteed Income Optimizer

Income-oriented GIC-related product.

  • Relevant for income-seeking customers.
  • Likely has narrower public discussion volume.
  • Lower priority for a mini analysis.

3. Savings Accounts

Scotia High Interest Savings Account (HISA)

Core deposit-rate comparison product.

  • Strong fit for competitor comparison against digital banks and major banks.
  • Clear sentiment around rate level, balance tiers, and promotional offers.
  • Easy for customers to understand and discuss publicly.

MomentumPLUS Savings Account

Savings rate depends on saving period and bonus structure.

  • Useful for tracking reaction to premium periods and bonus interest.
  • Sentiment may reveal whether customers find the rules attractive or complicated.
  • Good comparison point against simpler high-interest savings offers.

Savings Accelerator Account

Often connected to registered or investment accounts.

  • Relevant for TFSA, FHSA, RRSP, RESP, RRIF, and RDSP contexts.
  • Good fit if the project expands into registered savings behavior.
  • May be less visible in broad public sentiment than HISA or MomentumPLUS.

Money Master Savings Account

Savings product with Smart Savings tools.

  • Potential sentiment topic around bonus interest and savings habits.
  • Less central to rate comparison than HISA and MomentumPLUS.
  • Lower priority for the first dashboard version.

Foreign Currency Savings

USD and Euro savings accounts.

  • Relevant to foreign currency customers.
  • Potentially affected by FX and international rate context.
  • Too narrow for the initial mini analysis unless specifically requested.
