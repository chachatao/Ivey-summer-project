Scotiabank Interest Rate-Related Products Map

A structured overview of Scotiabank Canada product families where interest rates affect customer value, borrowing cost, competitive comparison, and potential market sentiment.

Scope Logic

Interest rate-related products are broader than mortgages. They include products where customers earn interest, pay interest, compare rates with competitors, or react to rate changes in the broader market.

Earn Interest Savings accounts, registered cash, investment cash, and business interest-bearing accounts.
Lock In Return GICs, cashable GICs, redeemable GICs, and market-linked GICs.
Pay Interest Mortgages, loans, auto financing, lines of credit, and credit cards.
Track Benchmarks Prime rate, reference rates, Bank of Canada rate, and US Federal Funds target rate.
